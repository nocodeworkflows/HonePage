Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

This policy covers Hone Growth and DemandTrack, the internal application we use to produce category demand reports. It explains what data we handle, and in particular how DemandTrack accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Who we are

Hone Growth is a growth marketing agency operating in the United Kingdom. For any question about this policy or about data we hold, contact hello@honegrowth.co.uk.

What DemandTrack does

DemandTrack connects to the Google Ads API to read aggregated keyword search volume, competition and bid-range data, together with account and geographic reference data. It uses that to produce demand and competitive reports for the brands we work with.

It is an internal tool. It is not offered for public sign-up, and it is used only with Google Ads accounts that we or our clients already control.

How we access Google user data

When an authorised person connects an account, DemandTrack uses Google OAuth 2.0. We request a single scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords — to read reporting and keyword planning data from Google Ads accounts that person already has access to.

We ask for no other Google scopes. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, Photos or any other Google service.

What we do and do not do with it

We use Google data only to:

  • retrieve aggregated keyword search volume, competition and bid-range metrics;
  • retrieve account, geographic and language reference data needed to make those calls;
  • build demand, trend and competitive reports for the account owner.

We do not:

  • create, change, pause or delete campaigns, ads, budgets or keywords;
  • sell, rent or trade Google user data;
  • use Google user data for advertising, profiling or to train machine learning models;
  • transfer Google user data to third parties except as set out below.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store, and for how long

  • OAuth tokens. An encrypted refresh token, so reports can be refreshed on a schedule without signing in each time. Held only while the connection is active.
  • Account identifiers. Google Ads customer IDs and account names, to know which account a report belongs to.
  • Aggregated metrics. Keyword search volumes, competition indices and bid ranges by geography and month. This is aggregated market data, not personal data.
  • Waitlist enquiries. If you email us to join the waitlist, your email address, until you ask us to remove it.

Aggregated metrics are retained as a time series for as long as we produce reports for that client. Tokens and account identifiers are deleted within 30 days of a connection being revoked or an engagement ending.

Who we share it with

We share Google user data with no one, other than the cloud infrastructure providers that host the application and its database on our behalf, under contract and only to the extent needed to run the service. We do not sell data. We disclose data to authorities only where legally required.

How it is protected

  • All traffic to Google APIs and to our own services uses TLS.
  • Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and never written to logs or source control.
  • Access is limited to the people who need it, on accounts with individual credentials.

Revoking access

You can disconnect DemandTrack at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing its access in your Google Ads account. Revoking stops all further data access immediately. Email us and we will delete anything already stored.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and may object to processing. Write to hello@honegrowth.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where we hold your contact details, tell you directly.
