The software

DemandTrack

DemandTrack is our internal application. It connects to the Google Ads API to read aggregated keyword search volume, competition and bid-range data, and combines it into category demand reports by geography for our clients.

It signs in with Google only to authorise access to Google Ads accounts our clients or our team already control, using the adwords scope. It reads reporting data. It does not create, edit or delete campaigns, and it is not available for public sign-up.

How we handle Google user data is set out in our privacy policy.
